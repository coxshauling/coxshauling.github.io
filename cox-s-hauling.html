What we haul

If it's in your way and headed out, we can probably take it — we load it up and haul it away. Cox's Hauling is not a moving company: we do not do whole-household moves, and we don't move furniture or belongings from one home to another. Every job is priced by the item, the walk, and the floors — no hourly rates.

  • Trash & Debris Removal

    Bagged trash, household clutter, and the pile in the corner of the garage — plus brush, yard debris, downed limbs and branches hauled away after storms or yard cleanups. Curbside or in-home pickup. One-time haul-offs only — no weekly trash route and no monthly pickup plan.

  • Clean Outs

    Garages, basements, sheds, whole rooms, and whole homes cleared out and hauled away — everything unwanted is loaded up and taken off, not moved to a new home.

  • Single Items

    One item picked up and hauled off — a couch, dresser, mattress and box spring set, armchair, or other single piece you're done with. Haul-off only — we don't move furniture to another home. Text a picture and ask.

  • Carpet Roll Haul-Away

    Old carpet and pad, rolled up and ready to go — we load it and haul it away for $50 a roll. We haul it, we don't pull it out: carpet must be rolled up before pickup. Full prices are in the carpet section below.

Fridges, freezers & AC units — strict rule, no exceptions: We do not take fridges, freezers, or AC units — big or small — unless the freon is cut out and the compressor is cut out completely. If both aren't already done, we can't take it. No exceptions.

Haul-away only — no household moves

Cox's Hauling takes away the stuff you don't want anymore — trash, cleanout loads, old furniture and mattresses as single items, brush, and yard debris. Once it's loaded, it's gone for disposal or donation. Fridges, freezers, and AC units are the one exception to ask about first: we take them only if the freon is cut out and the compressor is cut out completely — no exceptions.

We do not do whole-household moves. We don't pack up a home, and we don't carry furniture or belongings from one house to another. If you need something hauled off, text us. If you need a household moved, you'll need a moving company.
